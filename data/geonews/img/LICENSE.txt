Satellite pictures of Geo-News Weekly

The pictures in the week folders (<week>/<story>-<place>.jpg) are cut from the Sentinel-2 cloudless 2020 map:

  Sentinel-2 cloudless - https://s2maps.eu by EOX IT Services GmbH
  (Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2020)

The pictures were made from it by putting map tiles together and cutting them to the place of a story (geonews.py,
make_satellite). They are shared under the same licence: for non-commercial use, with the credit above.
