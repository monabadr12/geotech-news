Research highlights

  1. A state-of-the-art review of backfill grouting: recent developments, ground stability and future trends in shield tunnelling
    Tunnelling and Underground Space Technology
    Useful for shield crews and designers choosing grouting materials and timing, since it consolidates field-proven practice on controlling ground settlement behind the shield.
  2. Exploring large squeezing deformation treatment in the Haba Snow mountain tunnel from a practical perspective: lessons from failures and successes
    Tunnelling and Underground Space Technology
    Case-based lessons from both failed and successful squeezing-ground treatments give practitioners concrete guidance for handling large deformations in weak rock tunnels.
  3. Experimental Investigation of the Static-to-Kinetic Friction Transition of Piles in Sand Subjected to Various Loading Rates
    Journal of Geotechnical and Geoenvironmental Engineering
    Shows that pile capacity in sand drops noticeably with faster loading, so design and load-test interpretation should account for the rate at which loads are applied.
