Research highlights

  1. Load-displacement response of piles for jackets supporting offshore wind turbines from large scale tests and FE model
    Geomechanics for Energy and the Environment
    Combines large-scale pile tests with finite element checks for offshore wind jacket foundations, directly informing stiffness and load-displacement assumptions in design.
  2. Dynamic monitoring and prediction method for remedial grouting of in-service immersed tunnel sand foundation
    Underground Space
    Offers a way to monitor and predict how remedial grouting performs beneath an operating immersed tunnel, helping engineers judge when a sand foundation has been adequately stabilised.
  3. Extrapolation-induced reversal of apparent geosynthetic benefit in cyclic plate load testing
    Transportation Geotechnics
    Warns that extrapolating cyclic plate load test results can overstate geosynthetic benefit, so engineers should check test interpretation before crediting reinforcement in pavement design.
