How reports are linked. Every scan, Claude compares the day's new stories with the archive of the last 365 days and links a report to an earlier one only when both are about the same event, project or site, not merely a similar hazard in the same country, and notes the stage the story has reached. Without Claude, a cautious headline match links reports found within 30 days. Story cards across the site carry a Developing story chip that leads here. Check each report at its source: a link is a lead, not a confirmed connection.
