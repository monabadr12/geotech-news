How reports are linked. Every scan, Claude Sonnet 5.5 compares the day's new stories with the archive of the last 365 days and links a report to an earlier one only when both are about the same event, project or site, not merely a similar hazard in the same country, and notes the stage the story has reached. The cases it is unsure of, such as two place names in one region or a translated headline that may or may not name the same site, are read again by Claude Opus 5.5, each against its own closest earlier stories, and its decision stands. Without Claude, a cautious headline match links reports found within 30 days. Story cards across the site carry a Developing story chip that leads here. Check each report at its source: a link is a lead, not a confirmed connection.
